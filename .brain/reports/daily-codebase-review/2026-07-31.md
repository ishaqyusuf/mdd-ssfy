Inference: a malformed or unauthorized caller could attempt jobs outside the intended dispatch/packing flows unless upstream transport auth blocks all public tRPC calls.

- P1 - Customer payment portal identity is still account-number/phone keyed.
  Evidence: `apps/api/src/trpc/routers/customer.route.ts` keeps `getCustomerPayPortal` as `publicProcedure`; `apps/api/src/db/queries/customer.ts` accepts `accountNo`, maps `cust-<id>` to customer id, otherwise sets `query.phone = accountNo`, and returns pending sales plus customer/billing contact fields. ## Worker Usability Review

Production, dispatch, packing, and mobile worker surfaces are moving in the right direction: production material review is documented as nonblocking and mobile dispatch proof completion is now server-bound and retryable. The remaining worker risk is that broad task triggering is still exposed through a generic tRPC route while mobile uses it for dispatch and packing operations. Workers with mixed technical comfort need simple reliable buttons, but the platform should not require a generic public task pipe to make those buttons work.

Inventory remains the biggest trainability problem. The system has many useful states and safeguards, but Brain still says the reconciliation baseline regressed and repairs are stopped. Until the mismatch classes are resolved or explicitly product-scoped, warehouse and production staff cannot fully trust allocation/readiness messages as the single source of truth.

## Customer And Dealer Experience Review

Dealer experience has improved substantially: quote edit locks, request approval, next-step guidance, payment links, customer/internal PDF separation, and dealer/customer payment distinction are documented as complete. The next customer-facing gap is not guidance copy; it is proof and confidence. Dealers and customers need a simple pickup/delivery timeline with explicit evidence when an order is ready, picked up, delivered, paid, or waiting on GND review.

Customer payment experience remains risky because the staff/customer pay portal exposes pending sales and wallet/payment context by `accountNo` or phone-like lookup. The checkout v2 plan already covers tokenized checkout and logged-in customer depth, but the staff/customer overview pay portal should converge on a signed-link or authenticated-customer boundary before more self-service dashboard features expand.

## Door-Manufacturing Workflow Gaps

- Production readiness has strong package-level direction, but inventory drift still prevents a clean release claim for material allocation and fulfillment truth.
- Dealer quote/order guidance now separates pickup and delivery language, but there is no single proof timeline for dealer-visible manufacturing, pickup, delivery, customer-paid, and GND-paid milestones.
- Door/HPT/moulding/shelf parity remains split across new sales form and mobile plans. A practical operator preflight should highlight missing dimensions, swing/hinge/profile choices, supplier-sensitive size pricing, moulding row quantities, shelf category/product choices, and production-readiness blockers before save/approval/production handoff.
