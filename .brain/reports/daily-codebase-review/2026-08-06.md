# Daily GND Codebase Review - 2026-08-06

## Executive Summary

This read-only review used `.brain/` as the active Brain path because this workspace does not have a top-level `brain/` directory. The requested `apps/www` and `apps/expo-app` surfaces map to the current `apps/dashboard` and `apps/mobile` workspaces. I reviewed those, plus `apps/dealership`, `apps/api`, and the requested shared packages.

The highest current risk is still operational boundary drift, but today's strongest version is more specific than prior reports: the mobile dispatch proof-completion path is now protected and assignment-bound, while mobile dispatch start/cancel still goes through the generic public `taskTrigger` route. That means the safer protected dispatch route boundary is not consistently the operational path for drivers.

The second major risk is a contract mismatch in the newly documented customer-approved quantity-change workflow. Brain says release one creates a manual customer approval link and keeps the sale unchanged until token-scoped customer approval. Source now approves as an authenticated sales-rep action, returns no approval token, and has a regression test asserting no customer link. That is either stale Brain documentation or a product/financial approval bug; it needs a same-day decision because it affects customer trust and wallet-credit accountability.

The third major risk remains public payment/customer context. The customer pay portal is still account-number/phone keyed and returns pending sales, wallet balance, payment method, terminal context, and customer contact fields. Checkout/payment initialization and verification routes are also public. Some public surfaces are intentional for customer entry points, but the current boundary is too broad for revenue and customer data.

The worktree had one pre-existing source change before report writing: `packages/jobs/trigger.config.ts` adds `mode: "legacy"` to the Trigger Prisma extension. It was not changed by this review.

## Highest-Risk Findings

### P1 - Generic public task execution still bypasses protected operational routes

Evidence:

- `apps/api/src/trpc/routers/task-trigger.route.ts` exposes `taskTrigger.trigger` and `taskTrigger.status` as `publicProcedure`. `trigger` accepts arbitrary `taskName` and `payload`, calls `tasks.trigger(...)`, and `status` returns run output/error.
- `apps/api/src/trpc/routers/task-events.route.ts` exposes `list`, `get`, `update`, `history`, `runNow`, `runTest`, and `runStatus` as `publicProcedure`.
- `apps/mobile/src/features/dispatch/api/use-dispatch-actions.ts` still starts/cancels dispatch work through `useTaskTrigger({ taskName: "update-sales-control" })`, while `completeDispatchWithProof` uses the protected dispatch mutation.
- `apps/mobile/src/hooks/use-task-trigger.ts` calls `_trpc.taskTrigger.trigger` and `_trpc.taskTrigger.status`.

Impact:

This undermines the route-hardening contract for driver and dispatch workflows. A mixed-skill operational team needs the server to own "who can start/cancel/complete this trip"; task-name/payload dispatching makes accountability too easy to lose.

### P1 - Customer-approved sales adjustment contract does not match source behavior

Evidence:

- `.brain/features/in-form-sales-order-adjustments.md` says the live sale stays unchanged while approval is pending and the employee creates an expiring customer approval link.
- `.brain/decisions/ADR-045-in-form-customer-approved-sales-adjustments.md` says the order stays effective until a token-scoped customer approval is accepted.
- `apps/api/src/db/queries/new-sales-form-adjustments.ts` creates adjustments directly with `status: "APPROVED"`, `appliedById: userId`, triggers `apply-sales-order-adjustment`, and returns `approvalToken: null`.
- `apps/dashboard/src/components/forms/new-sales-form/new-sales-form.tsx` uses "Sales representative approved..." copy and submits the adjustment directly from the review sheet.
- `apps/dashboard/src/components/forms/new-sales-form/sections/sales-change-review-sheet.test.ts` explicitly asserts the UI does not contain "Create customer approval link", "Copy approval link", or "Customer contact reference".

Impact:

For paid orders and reductions that may create wallet credit, the current behavior appears to replace customer approval with employee approval despite the accepted ADR. That can create customer-trust, financial-dispute, and audit problems unless it is an intentional policy change and Brain is updated.

### P1 - Public customer payment surfaces expose and mutate too much payment context

Evidence:

- `apps/api/src/trpc/routers/customer.route.ts` exposes `getCustomerPayPortal` as `publicProcedure`.
- `apps/api/src/db/queries/customer.ts` resolves pending sales by `accountNo` or phone and returns `pendingSales`, `wallet`, `walletBalance`, `recentPaymentMethod`, Square terminal list/error context, and `lastTerminalId`.
- Pending sales include order ids, totals, amount due, customer/billing names, email, phone, and address.
- `apps/api/src/trpc/routers/checkout.route.ts` exposes checkout initialization, quote acceptance, checkout-link creation, payment verification, and device-code generation as `publicProcedure`.

Impact:

The customer-facing payment path needs a public entry point, but account/phone lookup plus broad payment and customer context is too loose for customer trust. Signed tokens, customer auth, or narrower one-time payment scopes would be easier to explain and train.

### P1 - Driver and warehouse read routes remain public while driver-platform plans require assigned-driver privacy

Evidence:

- `apps/api/src/trpc/routers/dispatch.route.ts` keeps `index`, `assignedDispatch`, `orderDispatchOverview`, `dispatchOverview`, `dispatchOverviewV2`, `packingQueue`, and `packingList` as `publicProcedure`.
- `.brain/plans/driver-platform-revival/map.md` explicitly marks dispatch reads as not ready and calls out public dispatch data exposure.
- `apps/api/src/trpc/routers/dispatch.route.ts` does protect start/cancel/proof-completion mutations, so the inconsistency is mostly on read/work-queue contracts plus task-trigger status changes.

Impact:

Drivers, warehouse staff, and installers should only see their assigned work or manager-scoped queues. Public read routes can expose customer names, addresses, delivery details, and operational timing outside the intended work queue.

### P2 - Office organization routes remain public despite an approved scoping plan

Evidence:

- `apps/api/src/db/queries/organization.ts` still defines `getOrganizationProfile` and `createOrganizationProfile` as `publicProcedure`.
- The plan `.brain/plans/2026-07-29-feature-office-organization-management-and-scoping.md` requires protected organization APIs, membership verification, active organization context, and a regression that organization routes do not use `publicProcedure`.

Impact:

Multi-office scoping will not be reliable if organization metadata and creation remain unauthenticated. This also blocks clean accountability for sales, projects, tasks, notifications, and future org-scoped analytics.

### P2 - Settings Active Sessions is still a mock table

Evidence:

- `apps/dashboard/src/components/user-logged-in-devices.tsx` renders hard-coded `mockDevices` for Chrome/macOS and Safari/iOS and simulates logout with `timeout(500)`.
- `apps/api/src/db/queries/user.ts` has a real `getLoggedInDevices` helper, but `rg` found no router wiring or UI use.

Impact:

Users and admins see a security-control surface that does not represent real active sessions and cannot revoke devices. That is confusing for office staff and weakens trust in login/security tooling.

### P2 - Runtime debug logs remain in production-adjacent mobile/payment/notification paths

Evidence:

- `packages/notifications/src/notification-trigger.ts` logs emitted notification payloads.
- `packages/notifications/src/index.ts` logs activity/contact and email result details.
- `apps/mobile/src/hooks/use-job-form.tsx` and `apps/mobile/src/hooks/use-job-form-2.tsx` log form changes and error variables.
- `packages/sales/src/storefront-account.ts` logs `{ devMode, e }`.

Impact:

These paths touch notifications, mobile job submission, storefront account setup, and potentially customer/employee data. Operational logs should be structured, environment-gated, and privacy-bounded.

## Worker Usability Review

Sales and office users now have a richer Sales Overview and new sales form, but the adjustment workflow currently says "sales representative approved" where Brain says "customer approved." For a mixed-skill office team, that distinction matters: staff need to know whether they are recording customer consent or personally authorizing a financial change.

Driver and warehouse workers benefit from the improved proof-completion form: recipient, signature, and photo attachments are in one mobile flow and the API owns retry/idempotency. However, start/cancel still runs through a generic background-task abstraction, and driver read routes remain public. Workers should not have to understand which button is protected by which backend route.

Inventory operators have meaningful UI progress in Sales Overview needs/inbounds, but the Brain ledger still keeps the correctness cutover open and repairs stopped. The product should keep labeling release readiness conservatively until reconciliation and operator proof gates are clean.

## Customer And Dealer Experience Review

The dealer portal now has stronger quote-to-order locks and next-step guidance. It separates the dealer's GND payable from the dealer's customer receivable, which is the right mental model for dealerships.

Dealer experience still lacks a manufacturing preflight: before the dealer requests an order, the app should call out missing or risky door details such as dimensions, hinge/swing/handing, profile/pricing readiness, delivery mode, and any line that GND will need to clarify.

The customer adjustment approval page exists, but current source does not create or return a customer approval token from the in-form flow. If release one is intentionally sales-rep-approved, the customer-facing page and Brain contract should be revised. If release one is intended to be customer-approved, this is a functional gap.

The customer pay portal remains the biggest customer-trust boundary issue. Public payment entry can be valid, but the current account/phone lookup returns too much sales, wallet, terminal, and contact context.

## Door-Manufacturing Workflow Gaps

- Dealer quote/order flows do not yet surface a door-manufacturing preflight for dimensions, handing/swing, profile, supplier, delivery/pickup, and production-readiness blockers before a quote is sent to GND.
- Driver/warehouse plans already identify missing structured manifest details: pre-hung rows need size plus explicit LH/RH/handing evidence or a "Handing not recorded" warning.
- Inventory correctness remains open, and the stopped repair state means production readiness should continue to display conservative, review-oriented language.
- Mobile dispatch completion captures proof, but inventory-backed driver completion and exact allocation consumption are still planned rather than complete.
- Barcode or scan-to-pack workflow is not evident in source or Brain as a current tracked item; photo proof exists, but scan proof would reduce warehouse mistakes for similar doors.
