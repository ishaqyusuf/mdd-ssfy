## Highest-Risk Findings

- P1 - Task-event scheduler controls are public mutations. Evidence: `apps/api/src/trpc/routers/task-events.route.ts` defines `list`, `get`, `update`, `history`, `runNow`, `runTest`, and `runStatus` all through `publicProcedure`. Inference: the `update`, `runNow`, and `runTest` routes can alter or trigger operational background tasks without a route-level authenticated Super Admin/operator boundary, which is high risk for payment reports, inventory jobs, notifications, and other scheduled work.
- P1 - Broad internal read/report routes still use public tRPC boundaries. Evidence: `apps/api/src/trpc/routers/filters.route.ts` exposes customer, dispatch, employee, inbound, inventory, job, contractor payout, product report, sales production, sales order, sales quote, sales accounting, project unit, and unit production filters via `publicProcedure`; `apps/api/src/trpc/routers/jobs.route.ts` exposes jobs, payment dashboard, payment portal, contractor payouts, payout print data, analytics, overview, and payment overview reads via `publicProcedure`; `apps/api/src/trpc/routers/inventories.route.ts` exposes inventory summary, low stock summary, inventory imports, product list, category form, inventory form, and variant stock form reads via `publicProcedure`. Inference: public reads across payroll, production, inventory, addresses, customer, and sales filters can leak operational context and make permission rules hard to reason about.
- P1 - Customer pay and checkout remain public while returning payment-operational context. Evidence: `apps/api/src/trpc/routers/customer.route.ts` exposes `getCustomerPayPortal` via `publicProcedure`; `apps/api/src/db/queries/sales-payment-processor.ts` uses `accountNo` to fetch pending sales and wallet context; `apps/api/src/trpc/routers/checkout.route.ts` exposes checkout initialization, quote acceptance, checkout-link creation, verification, and device-code generation via `publicProcedure`. Inference: tokenized checkout flows may legitimately be public, but account-number keyed payment views and public device/payment operations need a sharper signed-link/customer-auth boundary.

The failure spans permissions, customer forms, sales dispatch status, legacy sales form helpers/tests, payment terminal status, Zod resolver, task monitor, customer-statement tables, and shared UI React types.

## Worker Usability Review

Sales reps have better preflight support than earlier runs: production readiness checks and customer/address editing are now documented as complete. The remaining usability issue is hidden trust. A worker can see a polished role-aware UI while read APIs still use permissive route seams, making it unclear which data access is intentional.

Production, dispatch, warehouse, installers, and drivers benefit from the completed dispatch proof migration.
