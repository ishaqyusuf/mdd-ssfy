# U.S. Millwork Business Revenue Benchmark

## Question

What would a U.S. business comparable to GND Millwork roughly generate in a
year?

## Short Answer

A reasonable outside estimate is **$5 million-$10 million of annual gross
revenue**, with **about $6 million-$7 million** as a defensible base case.

Use **$3 million-$12 million** as the wider planning range when headcount, sales
mix, and order volume are unknown. A small shop can fall below that range,
while a builder-focused or wholesale-heavy operator can exceed it without
having many consumer-facing locations.

This is an estimate of **business sales/revenue**, not the owner's income,
salary, profit, cash flow, or company valuation. Revenue is the top line before
materials, payroll, rent, freight, insurance, taxes, and other expenses.

## Why GND Is a Hybrid

GND's official site presents one Miami/Kendall facility, describes the company
as a manufacturer supported by suppliers, and sells or quotes pre-hung doors,
door slabs, mouldings, and hardware. It also says the facility can produce
custom wood and fiberglass doors. That makes it a hybrid of manufacturing,
retail/dealer sales, and millwork distribution rather than a clean match to one
industry code. See the [official GND homepage](https://gndmillwork.com/) and
[official pre-hung door catalog](https://gndmillwork.com/product-category/interior-doors/prehung-doors/).

The closest 2022 NAICS industries are:

- `321911` — wood window and door manufacturing;
- `321918` — other millwork, including factory-made millwork;
- `337212` — custom architectural woodwork and millwork manufacturing;
- `444180` — other building-material dealers;
- `423310` — lumber, plywood, millwork, and wood-panel wholesalers.

## Census Benchmarks

The most useful primary source is the U.S. Census Bureau's 2022 Economic Census
firm-size table. The values below divide reported industry sales, value of
shipments, or revenue (`RCPTOT`, reported in thousands of dollars) by the
reported number of firms in each employee band. They are therefore **average
revenue per employer firm**, not medians.

| Employees per firm | Door manufacturing | Other millwork | Custom millwork | Building-material retail | Millwork wholesale |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 5-9 | $1.55M | $1.35M | $1.29M | $2.63M | $7.62M |
| 10-19 | $3.01M | $3.71M | $2.46M | $5.73M | $11.89M |
| 20-49 | $7.02M | $8.39M | $5.60M | $13.50M | $30.59M |

Sources: [Census EC2200SIZEEMPFIRM table](https://data.census.gov/table/ECNSIZE2022.EC2200SIZEEMPFIRM?q=EC2200SIZEEMPFIRM),
[official bulk data file](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/economic-census/data/2022/sector00/EC2200SIZEEMPFIRM.zip),
and the Census Bureau's
[2022 Economic Census data-dissemination guide](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/economic-census/year/2022/guidance/how-2022-ecdata-disseminated.html).

The spread is meaningful. A manufacturer earns revenue from what it makes; a
dealer or wholesaler can move much more product per employee because purchased
goods pass through revenue. A GND-like company combining shop production with
supplied brands, office sales, dealer accounts, delivery, and a storefront
should therefore be benchmarked above a pure custom cabinet/millwork shop but
below a high-throughput wholesaler unless its contractor volume is unusually
large.

## Practical Range by Business Scale

| Comparable scale | Rough annual gross revenue | Interpretation |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Small local shop, usually under 10 employees | **$1M-$3M** | Fits the manufacturing and building-material retail averages; a wholesale-heavy shop can be higher. |
| Established single-location operator, roughly 10-25 employees | **$3M-$12M** | Best broad comparison for a GND-like production, office, dealer, and storefront hybrid. |
| Established regional operator, roughly 20-49 employees | **$7M-$20M** | Supported by manufacturing and dealer averages; a strong wholesale/builder mix can reach roughly $30M. |
| Multi-location regional platform | **$20M+** | Revenue becomes highly dependent on branch count, builder contracts, and the share of distribution versus custom work. |

The scale ranges are a reasoned synthesis of the Census averages, not reported
GND results. The base case of **$6M-$7M** places the business near the
10-19-employee retail benchmark and the lower portion of the 20-49-employee
door/millwork manufacturing benchmarks. The broader **$5M-$10M** shorthand is
appropriate when the business appears established but reliable headcount and
financial statements are unavailable.

## Large-Company Sanity Check

Builders FirstSource is not a direct comparable, but its filings show where the
same product family goes at national scale. The company says its facilities
assemble pre-hung doors and produce custom millwork; for 2025 it reported
**$3.8362 billion** of windows, doors, and millwork net sales and approximately
**585 locations** overall. This is useful only as an upper-scale reference, not
as a valuation or per-location benchmark for GND. See the
[Builders FirstSource 2025 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1316835/000119312526054643/bldr-20251231.htm).

## Important Limits

- The Census figures are 2022 nominal dollars and cover employer firms.
- The values are industry averages, not medians; a few large or
  wholesale-oriented firms can pull an average upward.
- Employee count and revenue mix matter more than having an office, dealer
  portal, or website by themselves.
- A reliable company-specific estimate would need at least annual order count,
  average order value, employee count, contractor-versus-consumer mix, and the
  portion of revenue from resale versus in-house production.
- Nothing in this report estimates net profit or owner compensation.

## Bottom Line

For a U.S. business with GND's apparent one-facility footprint and hybrid
manufacturer/dealer/storefront model, use **roughly $5M-$10M per year in gross
revenue**, with **$6M-$7M as the base case** and **$3M-$12M as the cautious
outside range**.
