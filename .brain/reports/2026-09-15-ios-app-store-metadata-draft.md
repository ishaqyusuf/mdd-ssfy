# GND Millwork App Store metadata draft

Date: 2026-09-15
Status: Draft for product/legal review; nothing saved in App Store Connect

## Positioning boundary

The binary is publicly discoverable, but the product is an authenticated
business operations app for existing GND company accounts. It has no public
self-registration. Do not describe it as a consumer shopping app or promise
features that are not available to the review account's role.

## Draft English (U.S.) listing

### Subtitle (30-character limit)

`Company operations on the go`

### Promotional text (optional, 170-character limit)

Leave blank for version 1.0 unless the product owner approves time-sensitive
copy. This field can be changed without a new version and should not be used to
hide required feature or access explanations.

### Description (4,000-character limit)

> GND Millwork gives authorized company members secure mobile access to the
> workflows assigned to their role.
>
> Depending on your company permissions, you can review assigned work, follow
> job or installation activity, coordinate dispatch and warehouse packing,
> access relevant documents, receive operational notifications, and keep the
> app current with managed updates.
>
> Access requires an existing active GND company account. Features and data
> are permission-based, so the screens available to each team member may
> differ. This app does not offer public account registration.

This copy is source-supported by protected role routing for Sales, Jobs,
Installers, Dispatch/Drivers, Warehouse Packing, Documents, Notifications,
Settings and Updates. Product owner must confirm that every named workflow is
ready for the first public binary and remove any that should not be advertised.

### Keywords (100-character limit)

`millwork,operations,dispatch,installation,jobs,warehouse,documents,employees`

Do not add competitor names, unverifiable superlatives, or consumer-shopping
keywords. The owner may replace terms after search-positioning review.

## Screenshot capture plan

Capture from the exact release candidate with a purpose-built demo company
account and synthetic/non-customer records. Never expose production employee,
customer, address, phone, document, signature, order or notification data.

Suggested truthful sequence, adjusted to the demo account's actual grants:

1. Sign-in screen, showing that an existing company account is required.
2. The role-specific landing screen with synthetic assigned work.
3. One representative job/installation or dispatch workflow.
4. A safe document or activity/status view using synthetic content.
5. Notifications or Settings, including the in-app Privacy Policy link.

Provide both the required iPhone and configured iPad sizes. Validate the iPad
layout before capture; do not reuse stretched phone artwork. App previews are
optional and should remain absent unless an authentic, reviewed recording is
available.

## App Review information draft

### Sign-in

- Keep **Sign-in required** checked.
- Username: owner-created, least-privilege review account; enter only in App
  Store Connect at the separately confirmed credential-transmission gate.
- Password: unique review-account password; never place it in Git, Brain,
  `.env*`, EAS public variables, screenshots, or chat.
- The account must remain active for the entire review and expose enough
  representative, synthetic data for Apple to evaluate the app.

### Review notes

> GND Millwork is publicly downloadable but is intended for authorized company
> members. The app does not provide public registration. Use the review account
> supplied in the Sign-In Information section.
>
> Available screens are determined by the authenticated user's company role
> and permissions. The supplied account is configured with representative
> access and synthetic review data. No hardware accessory, purchase, or
> App Store Connect membership is required to use the reviewed workflows.
>
> If assistance is needed during review, contact the person listed in App
> Review Contact Information.

Before saving, add concise navigation steps for the exact release candidate
and review account. Do not claim a workflow that the account cannot reach.

### Contact information

The Account Holder must provide a monitored first name, last name, phone and
email. These are operational personal/contact data sent to Apple and are not
appropriate to infer from source or store in this packet.
