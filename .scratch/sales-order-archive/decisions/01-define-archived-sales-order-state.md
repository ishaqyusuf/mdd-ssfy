# Define Archived Sales Order State And Lifecycle Boundaries

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: None
Parent: [`../map.md`](../map.md)

## Question

What exactly makes a Sales Order archived, which orders are eligible to enter
that state, and which existing meanings must remain unchanged across Sales Bin,
lifecycle status, direct order access, and operational queues?

## Comments

> *This was generated by AI during Wayfinder issue commenting.*

**Proposed answer:** An Archived Sales Order is a non-deleted Sales Order with a
nullable archive timestamp. Archive is reversible workspace visibility, not
Sales Bin deletion or a payment, production, dispatch, fulfillment, inventory,
or accounting status. Any non-deleted order may be archived, but archiving
non-terminal work must warn that operational work continues. Archived orders
remain accessible through direct links and purpose-built operational queues.
Restore clears the archive state; deletion remains independently represented by
`deletedAt` and Sales Bin.

**Why:** This supports tidying without corrupting lifecycle truth or hiding work
from operational systems.

**Assumptions / risk:** Allowing non-terminal orders to be archived is useful
for manual cleanup but requires a clear warning.
