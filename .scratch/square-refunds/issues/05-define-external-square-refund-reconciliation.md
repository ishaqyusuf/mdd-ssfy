# Define External Square Refund Reconciliation

Type: grilling
Status: closed
Blocked by: 01, 02, 03
Parent: [`../map.md`](../map.md)

## Question

How should GND authenticate, deduplicate, ingest, classify, and reconcile Square
refunds created through Square Dashboard, POS, Terminal, or another Square
application before Finance supplies explicit Sales Order allocations and local
balances are allowed to change?

## Comments

> *This was generated by AI during Wayfinder issue commenting.*

**Proposed answer:** Authenticate Square webhooks from the raw request body and
exact callback URL, durably deduplicate them by event ID, and upsert refunds by
Square refund ID. Process events asynchronously and reconcile the latest
provider resource because delivery may be duplicated, delayed, or out of
order.

A refund not initiated by GND enters `EXTERNAL` origin and
`AWAITING_ALLOCATION`. Its amount immediately reduces remaining
provider-refundable capacity, but it does not change local Sales Order
balances. Finance must allocate the completed amount only among orders funded
by the verified original tender, with explicit principal/C.C.C/tip
classification and audit evidence. The allocation total must equal the
provider amount.

If GND cannot resolve the tender or determine valid allocations, keep an
unresolved Finance exception and never guess. Use overlapping, paginated
`ListPaymentRefunds` reconciliation plus targeted `GetPaymentRefund` reads to
recover missed events. Historical pre-launch results remain report-only.

**Why:** This reconciles refunds made through Square Dashboard, POS, Terminal,
or other applications without corrupting Sales balances.

**Assumptions / risk:** Square POS notes may not appear in webhook data, so
Finance may need external evidence before allocating some refunds.

### Resolution

Implemented raw-body/exact-URL signature verification, event dedupe, asynchronous
provider convergence, external `awaiting_allocation`, Finance review and exact
eligible-order allocation, plus overlapping scheduled list/get reconciliation.
