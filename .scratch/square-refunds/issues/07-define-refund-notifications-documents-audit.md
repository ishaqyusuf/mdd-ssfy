# Define Refund Notifications Documents And Audit

Type: grilling
Status: closed
Blocked by: 03, 04, 05
Parent: [`../map.md`](../map.md)

## Question

What exact customer, initiator, Finance, Sales Activity, payment-history,
invoice-summary, document-refresh, and immutable evidence contract should each
Square Refund state produce without exposing internal failure details or
rewriting original invoice principal?

## Comments

> *This was generated by AI during Wayfinder issue commenting.*

**Proposed answer:** Treat every provider and local-application transition as
immutable audit evidence, including actor, origin, payment/refund IDs,
idempotency identity, amounts, allocations, commercial reference, safe provider
status/error details, event IDs, and timestamps.

Communication rules:

- Requested or provider-pending: internal Sales Activity only; do not tell the
  customer that the refund succeeded.
- Pending beyond 24 hours: notify initiator and Finance; repeat escalation at
  7 days and mark critical at Square's 14-day support boundary.
- Completed and locally applied: notify the customer, initiator, assigned
  salesperson, and Finance. Customer copy states that Square completed the
  refund and the bank may still take 7-10 business days to display it.
- Failed or rejected: notify initiator and Finance with a safe resolution
  summary; do not send a success-style customer email.
- External/unallocated: notify Finance and retain the exception until
  allocation is complete.

After local application, refresh every affected invoice/payment summary and
show the completed refund plus resulting balance while preserving original
invoice principal. Pending and failed attempts remain internal. Delivery must
be idempotent per refund transition.

**Why:** This prevents premature customer claims while giving Sales and Finance
complete operational evidence.

**Assumptions / risk:** The 24-hour and 7-day alert thresholds are recommended
operational defaults and should remain configurable if production experience
requires adjustment.

### Resolution

Implemented immutable transition evidence, Sales Activity attribution,
24-hour/7-day/14-day internal escalation, safe pending/failure messages,
customer completion email, initiator/Finance completion notices, idempotent
document refresh, and preserved invoice principal.
