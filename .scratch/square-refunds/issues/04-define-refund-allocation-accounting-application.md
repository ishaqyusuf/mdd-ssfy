# Define Refund Allocation And Accounting Application

Type: grilling
Status: closed
Blocked by: 01, 03
Parent: [`../map.md`](../map.md)

## Question

How should one completed Square Refund apply its frozen principal, C.C.C, tip,
and multi-order allocations to canonical and compatibility payment ledgers,
Sales Order paid/due projections, Finance exceptions, and idempotent downstream
rebuilds without changing commercial or operational lifecycle state?

## Comments

> *This was generated by AI during Wayfinder issue commenting.*

**Proposed answer:** Freeze all values in integer cents. Each affected Sales
Order receives an explicit principal allocation; C.C.C and tip remain separate
payment-level components. The sum of order principal allocations, refundable
C.C.C, and refundable tip must equal the Square refund total.

When Square reports `COMPLETED`, one idempotent transaction should:

- Post one canonical refund ledger entry identified by the Square refund ID.
- Apply the frozen principal allocations to their respective Sales Orders.
- Record C.C.C, tip, and retained Square fees separately without adding them to
  order principal.
- Create the required compatibility negative Sales Payment projections per
  order.
- Rebuild each affected paid/due projection.
- Create a Finance exception when a refund reopens balance on a completed or
  fulfilled order.
- Queue document, activity, and notification work through an after-commit
  outbox.

Do not create a Wallet Credit transaction or use the disconnected historical
refund graph as a second authority. The original commercial total and
operational status remain unchanged.

**Why:** This keeps canonical accounting, legacy compatibility views, and
multi-order balances consistent.

**Assumptions / risk:** Existing Finance reads must be migrated or adapted to
the canonical refund projection during the same cutover; otherwise different
screens will continue reporting different totals.

### Resolution

Implemented exact principal/C.C.C./tip and multi-order allocation, one
canonical completion ledger, per-order negative compatibility projections,
balance rebuilds, Finance resolution exceptions, activity, document refresh,
and after-commit notifications without changing commercial lifecycle state.
