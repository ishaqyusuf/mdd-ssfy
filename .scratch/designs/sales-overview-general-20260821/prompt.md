# Improved design prompt

Create five high-fidelity, production-feasible redesign directions for the existing GND Sales Overview General tab inside its 672px desktop primary sheet.

Use the real order `09397LM` data. Preserve the header, tab bar, Preview/Edit/More actions, status selector, Special Order state, customer/order/sales-rep facts, payment summary, Invoice Details, fixed footer, and secondary-pane behavior. Do not add features, change data, or turn the sheet into a marketing dashboard.

Optimize for internal sales staff who need to identify the customer, understand the order state, reconcile the payment state, and find the next action in under five seconds. Use a calm, professional, operational visual language; strong hierarchy; flat sections or dividers instead of card soup; obvious actions; accessible contrast; and responsive layouts at 390px and 672px.

Each direction must use a distinct layout model, font family, and restrained palette, and must remain feasible in the project's React, Tailwind, shadcn, and Midday-style architecture.
