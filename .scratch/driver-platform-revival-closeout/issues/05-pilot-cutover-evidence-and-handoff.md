# 05 — Produce pilot, cutover, and handoff evidence

**What to build:** Assemble the operational proof needed to decide whether the
revived driver platform can enter a controlled pilot. Validate the final native
flow on representative Android layouts, reconcile inventory outcomes, document
rollback and support procedures, and align the project record with the proven
state.

**Blocked by:** 04 — Prove lifecycle failure safety and reconciliation.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Expo Go QA passes on a supported phone viewport and a tablet or
      landscape-class viewport for cold start, warm cache, deep link, refresh,
      background/restore, weak network, and proof retry.
- [ ] Packing controls remain reachable above persistent footers; keyboards,
      modals, scrolling, text scaling, touch targets, labels, contrast, and
      screen-reader order are verified.
- [ ] The approved near-black, charcoal, neutral-text, and green semantic theme
      remains consistent across login, queue, manifest, packing, trip, proof,
      settings, loading, empty, error, and offline states.
- [ ] Permission evidence covers unauthenticated, assigned-driver,
      cross-driver, warehouse-operator, manager, and non-development quick-login
      behavior.
- [ ] Before/after reconciliation evidence proves exact picked and consumed
      quantities for representative inventory-only and mixed dispatches.
- [ ] The runbook documents fixture use, feature/eligibility controls,
      reconciliation, known failure messages, support ownership, rollback
      thresholds, and the rule that consumed stock requires audited repair.
- [ ] A client-ready recording or screenshot sequence demonstrates due context,
      structured item facts, truthful packing, readiness, Start Trip, and proof
      completion.
- [ ] Brain feature, plan, decision, API, permission, database, task, and progress
      records are checked and updated to match the actual implementation and
      evidence.
- [ ] The final handoff distinguishes completed implementation, verified pilot
      gates, deferred production rollout, and explicit out-of-scope work.
- [ ] No production mutation, synchronization, cohort expansion, or legacy
      removal occurs without a separate approved rollout action.
