# Define The One-Business-Day Manager Escalation

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 01, 02, 03, 04
Parent: [`../map.md`](../map.md)

## Question

How is one business day measured for an unresolved Sales Handoff Action, and
how does the in-app escalation reach the correct Super Admins without duplicate
or stale notifications? Define the source timestamp, New York business calendar
and weekends, action changes, deduplication identity, acknowledgement,
resolution, reopening, deep link, responsible-representative evidence, and
activity history.

Email and push are not part of the first release. The notification must not
grant approval or operational authority by itself.

## Comments

> *This was generated by AI during Wayfinder issue commenting.*

**Proposed answer:** Start an action epoch when a Sales Handoff Action first
becomes visible under the current policy. Escalate once after one New York
business day, measured as the same local time on the next weekday; weekends are
skipped and holiday calendars are excluded initially.

- A policy change that exposes existing orders starts new epochs at the
  policy-change time.
- Material and Production actions use separate identities.
- Notify every active Super Admin once through the in-app notification system.
- Deduplicate by order, action type, and action epoch.
- Resolution cancels pending escalation.
- A later genuine reopening creates a new epoch and may escalate again.
- Acknowledgement records that the notification was seen but does not resolve
  the action.
- Sales-representative transfer updates ownership while preserving the ongoing
  action’s clock.
- The notification includes current representative, order, action type, opened
  time, and the same protected deep link as the alert.
- Resolution, reopening, acknowledgement, and escalation remain visible in
  activity history.

**Why:** Managers receive one actionable escalation without storms,
retroactive floods, or confusing acknowledgement with completion.

**Assumptions / risk:** Weekday-only business-day calculation is acceptable
until GND adopts a formal holiday calendar. Email and push remain excluded from
the first release.
