# Define Nonblocking Production And Packing Review

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 02
Parent: [`../map.md`](../map.md)

## Question

How should production workers and packing actors record physically observed
work when material evidence is missing, stale, or unresolved? Define the shared
and distinct report grains, actor scope, quantity rules, idempotency, evidence
snapshot, pending-review state, duplicate prevention, downstream holds,
approval, rejection, recheck, expiry, and revision behavior.

The answer must explicitly reconcile ADR-062's production-only worker block and
the current packing rejection of pending production review. Approval must use
canonical inventory, production-review, and packing commands; a generic
availability rewrite or blanket bypass is not acceptable.

## Comments

> *This was generated by AI during Wayfinder issue commenting.*

**Proposed answer:** Allow authenticated actors to record physically observed
work under a revision-bound pending review while retaining domain-specific
production and packing records.

- Production-only workers may submit against their own active assignments when
  material evidence is missing, unavailable, or unresolved. This supersedes
  ADR-062’s hard rejection and routes the quantity through ADR-039 review.
- Pending production quantity counts as reported for duplicate prevention but
  not as finalized production, payroll, packing eligibility, dispatch,
  fulfillment, or payment review.
- A packing actor may report physically verified packed quantity when upstream
  system evidence is stale. It remains reported packing—not canonical packed
  quantity—and cannot authorize loading or dispatch.
- A genuine physical shortage cannot be reported as packed; it uses the
  existing shortage/exception workflow.
- Bind every report to authenticated actor, order/assignment or dispatch
  allocation, exact quantity, evidence revision, and idempotency key.
- Fresh canonical recheck, receiving, manual fulfillment, or production-review
  resolution may approve the report. Rejection removes or voids pending
  reported quantity without fabricating inventory.
- Relevant order, allocation, assignment, or evidence changes make the review
  stale and require re-evaluation; elapsed time alone does not approve or expire
  it.
- Production and packing share the review-envelope invariants but retain
  separate domain commands and downstream effects.

**Why:** Real work is not lost because administration is stale, while
inventory, payroll, production, packing, and dispatch truth remain protected.

**Assumptions / risk:** ADR-062 must be superseded explicitly, and packing
requires a new domain-specific pending-report authority rather than reusing
production rows. This is the principal architecture decision in the map and
will require an ADR update.
