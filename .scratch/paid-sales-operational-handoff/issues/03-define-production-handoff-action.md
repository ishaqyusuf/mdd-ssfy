# Define The Production Handoff Action

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: None
Parent: [`../map.md`](../map.md)

## Question

What exact item and quantity evidence opens and resolves a Production Handoff
Action? Define production-capable item selection, active owned assignments,
partial assignment, multiple workers, unowned assignments, cancelled or
superseded assignments, order revisions, non-produceable lines, completed work,
terminal orders, and concurrent assignment changes.

The answer must treat material readiness as independent and must not consider a
production handoff complete while required quantity remains unowned.

## Comments

> *This was generated by AI during Wayfinder issue commenting.*

**Proposed answer:** Open a Production Handoff Action when a payment-qualified
order has production-capable required quantity not covered by an active owned
assignment or attributable completed production evidence.

- Aggregate assignment quantity across workers without double-counting
  overlapping or superseded rows.
- Partial assignment leaves the uncovered quantity actionable.
- Active unowned assignments do not satisfy the handoff.
- Cancelled, deleted, superseded, or stale-revision assignments are excluded.
- Completed owned assignments or finalized submissions continue to satisfy
  already-produced quantity.
- Order revisions open a new action only for new or changed required quantity
  that lacks coverage.
- Non-production items and terminal or cancelled orders are excluded.
- Material readiness is never part of this predicate.
- Every assignment mutation re-evaluates the action server-side.

**Why:** Assignment ownership—not inventory readiness or generic order
status—is the evidence that production received the work.

**Assumptions / risk:** Historical completed work must remain attributable
after its active assignment closes.
