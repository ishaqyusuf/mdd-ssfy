# Prototype The Paid Sales Action Alert

Type: prototype
Status: open
Blocked by: 04, 06
Parent: [`../map.md`](../map.md)

## Question

Does one low-cost prototype make the shadcn `Paid sales need action` alert clear
and usable for a sales representative with a few actions and a Super Admin with
many actions? Prototype the alert immediately before the Sales Orders table,
wrapping Material and Production pills, representative context, overflow,
loading, failure, resolved, narrow-screen, keyboard, and screen-reader states.

Each pill must demonstrate its exact Sales Overview deep link and the prototype
must establish whether the alert needs grouping, truncation, or an expanded
review surface before the final specification. This is a decision artifact, not
production implementation.

## Comments

> *This was generated by AI during Wayfinder issue commenting.*

**Proposed answer:** Prototype one standard shadcn `Alert` immediately before
the Sales Orders table:

- Title: `Paid sales need action`
- Description: wrapping, button-semantic badge pills such as `#09388PC —
  Material` and `#09388PC — Production`
- Material uses a restrained amber treatment; Production uses a restrained blue
  treatment.
- Sales representatives see a flat oldest-first list.
- Super Admins see the same initial list, with representative identity in the
  accessible label and tooltip; `Show all` expands an inline list grouped by
  representative.
- Display up to 12 pills initially and preserve the full total as `Show N more`.
- Narrow screens wrap pills vertically without horizontal scrolling.
- Pills are keyboard reachable, have visible focus, and announce order, action,
  and representative.
- Loading uses a compact skeleton; read failure shows `Unable to load paid sales
  actions` with Retry.
- Opening and closing Sales Overview returns focus to the originating pill.
- Resolved actions disappear after server invalidation without dismiss
  controls.

Accept the prototype when representative and Super-Admin scenarios remain
scannable at desktop and 390px width and both deep links open the correct Sales
Overview state.

**Why:** It validates density, overflow, accessibility, and manager context
without redesigning the Sales table.

**Assumptions / risk:** The 12-pill threshold and colors may be adjusted after
the prototype review.
