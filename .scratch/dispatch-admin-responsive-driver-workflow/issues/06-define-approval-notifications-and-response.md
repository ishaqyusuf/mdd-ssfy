# Define Approval Notifications And Response Workflow

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 04
Parent: [`../map.md`](../map.md)

## Question

How should a driver approval request reach the correct office admins through
the in-app notification area and email without duplicates or notification
storms? Define recipients, office scope, subject/payload, deep link, urgency,
deduplication, acknowledgement, escalation, approve/deny feedback, driver
status updates, expiry, and activity/audit history.

The notification must point to a protected review surface; email must not act
as an unauthenticated approval capability.
