# Define Approval Notifications And Response Workflow

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 04
Parent: [`../map.md`](../map.md)

## Question

How should a driver approval request reach the correct office admins through
the in-app notification area and email without duplicates or notification
storms? Define recipients, office scope, subject/payload, deep link, urgency,
deduplication, acknowledgement, escalation, approve/deny feedback, driver
status updates, expiry, and activity/audit history.

The notification must point to a protected review surface; email must not act
as an unauthenticated approval capability.

## Comments

> *This was generated by AI during Wayfinder issue commenting.*

**Proposed answer:** A driver reports a blocker against a specific dispatch,
item/allocation, blocker class, and evidence revision. Create one durable request
and one outbox event; notify office-scoped admins with the required capability
in-app immediately and by deduplicated email. Both channels deep-link to the
same authenticated review surface.

The admin resolves the underlying production, inbound, allocation, or
configuration problem, or approves only a revision-bound exception class that
policy permits. A genuine physical shortage cannot be made ready by a status
rewrite. The driver sees `Waiting for admin`, then receives an in-app
`Ready to resume` update when fresh server validation confirms the blocker is
cleared. Acknowledgement, decision, expiry, escalation, and every retry belong
in activity history; email never performs the approval itself.

**Why:** This removes the phone-call dependency without turning notification or
approval into a safety bypass or a source of duplicate emails.
