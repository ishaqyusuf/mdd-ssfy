# Define Packing Quantity Semantics

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: None
Parent: [`../map.md`](../map.md)

## Question

What is the exact row and quantity grain the driver verifies: sales door/item,
shipment line, inventory component, or a grouped presentation backed by more
than one of these? Define requested, produced, physically available, packed,
short, remaining, and previously delivered quantities; partial-packing rules;
whether zero is allowed; and how a reduced shipped quantity affects the current
dispatch, later backorder/reattempt work, customer documents, and the unchanged
commercial Sales Order.

The answer must preserve split-delivery allocation ownership and distinguish a
physical shortage from stale production or inbound administration.

## Comments

> *This was generated by AI during Wayfinder issue commenting.*

**Proposed answer:** Present customer-meaningful order items, but persist each
entry against the current dispatch allocation line. Show ordered quantity,
allocated to this dispatch, packed now, delivered previously, and remaining.
Allow a zero packed quantity only with a required blocker reason.

Before a dispatch leaves, use `Packing blocked` or `Missing items` for an item
that cannot be packed. Reserve `Back order` for the order-level exception that
exists after a partial dispatch is committed while commercial quantity remains
outstanding. Partial packing must reduce only the current dispatch quantity and
create or preserve remaining fulfillment demand; it must not rewrite the Sales
Order quantity or fabricate production/inventory evidence.

**Why:** This distinguishes a temporary warehouse blocker from a genuine
post-dispatch back order and remains correct across multiple dispatches.
