# Define Compatibility, Cutover, And Rollback

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 10
Parent: [`../map.md`](../map.md)

## Question

How should the approved responsive flow coexist with and eventually replace or
retain the current Dispatch Admin implementation, `/sales/packing-list`,
`/sales-book/dispatch-task`, and Expo driver experience? Define reused domain
commands, URL/deep-link compatibility, role rollout, pilot cohort, data or
schema needs, telemetry, acceptance evidence, rollback conditions, and the
explicit decision required before any old surface is removed.

The answer must reconcile this map with the existing Driver Platform Revival
Closeout rather than allowing two conflicting cutover programs to proceed.

## Comments

> *This was generated by AI during Wayfinder issue commenting.*

**Proposed answer:** Treat the responsive flow as a staged continuation of the
Driver Platform Revival, not a parallel authority. Reuse the current dispatch,
Packing List, inventory allocation, exception, and proof commands and preserve
`OrderDelivery`; do not duplicate operational data or lifecycle writes.

Roll out behind office/role/driver cohort controls. Keep Expo,
`/sales-book/dispatch-task`, and `/sales/packing-list` available during the
pilot, and preserve URL/deep-link compatibility through redirects or adapters.
Begin with a small office-admin and driver cohort, then measure manifest load
reliability, action failures, time to pack, blocker acknowledgement/resolution,
proof retries, completion success, fallback usage, stale-revision rejection,
and inventory reconciliation.

Expand only after assigned-work, packing, notification, proof, permission,
accessibility, weak-network, and reconciliation acceptance passes with no open
high-severity correctness or authorization defect. Change the canonical driver
surface through an explicit ADR. Redirect or retire an old surface only after a
separate product/architecture decision supported by sustained usage and parity
evidence. Packing List may remain the warehouse/admin workspace even if the
driver presentation changes.

Rollback disables the cohort or restores the previous route; it never reverses
completed dispatch, proof, allocation, inventory, or audit records. Any new
schema must be additive and backward-compatible until the rollback window
closes.

**Why:** This creates one controlled migration program and preserves completed
operational truth regardless of which interface is active.

**Assumptions / risk:** Feature completeness alone is not sufficient retirement
evidence. Cohort telemetry, operational feedback, and reconciliation must be
available before legacy removal.
