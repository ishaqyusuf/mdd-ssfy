# Define Policy, C.C.C, And Footer Presentation

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: None
Parent: [`../map.md`](../map.md)

## Question

How should customer-facing approval, HTML, and PDF surfaces hide the policy
version label while retaining immutable internal version evidence, place the
policy beside the document price footer, and use the canonical Sales C.C.C
calculation so every displayed total agrees with the Sales Order?

## Comments

> *This was generated by AI during Wayfinder issue commenting.*

**Proposed answer:** Customer-facing approval, HTML, and PDF surfaces display
**Policy** without `v1` or another version suffix. Settings, history, evidence,
and audit records retain immutable policy versions. Customer invoice/order
documents place the full policy in the footer's left column, with existing
notes beneath it, beside the right-column subtotal/price summary. Operational
documents retain compact Special Order status.

Approval-request issuance repairs C.C.C through the canonical Sales calculation
before freezing the snapshot. Card/link/terminal orders display the base
principal, `C.C.C (percentage)`, and `totalWithCcc`; non-card orders omit C.C.C.
`grandTotal` remains C.C.C-exclusive accounting principal. Relevant
payment-method, percentage, C.C.C, or displayed-total changes invalidate
approval.

**Why:** Public approval, Sales Order, HTML preview, and PDF must show identical
customer-payable totals without duplicating payment logic.

**Assumptions / risk:** Actual charged C.C.C and partial-payment history remain
owned by payment transaction metadata and are not rewritten into the order
principal.
